What happens on each push

  1. 1PressThe rod drives the rubber plug off its seat. Water flows to the spout.
  2. 2Hydraulic delayThe spring pushes the piston back up, but water must squeeze through the pinhole, so it rises slowly.
  3. 3Spring-return sealThe plug meets the seat and line pressure holds it shut. Flow stops.
